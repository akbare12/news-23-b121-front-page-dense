One image summing up 23 November 2026's world news: the AI rout hitting Asian
markets, the UN chief's call to regulate AI before it's too late, and the US lead
over China at a record low — plus 28 more stories, a world map, and a date check
that keeps 22 November's news out.

- **Entry image:** `entry.png` (1024×1024, generated by `draw.py` with Pillow)
- **Runner:** `main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` after validating the PNG header by hand.
- **31 stories:** 3 hero scenes, 10 cards, 18 more in "ALSO ON MONDAY".
- **Date check:** stories dated 23 Nov only. Left out: Dems reach 258 (22 Nov),
  Sparta II fire (22 Nov), El Chapo Isidro captured (22 Nov), EU–Indonesia pact
  (21 Nov), Nigeria boat (21 Nov), ICJ Myanmar ruling (19 Nov).

## Run

```sh
python3 draw.py            # regenerates entry.png
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # validates + copies entry.png to out/
```
